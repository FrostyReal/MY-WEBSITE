Ultimate Pirate Championship

A Sea of Thieves event where you choose your opponent, the rules, and the game mode.

Live events happen on Saturday nights.

Join the Discord Watch on YouTube

How it works

  1. Choose your opponent. Pick who you want to fight.
  2. Choose the rules and game mode. You decide how the fight is played.
  3. Fight on Saturday night. Live events stream on the UPC YouTube channel and on Kick.
